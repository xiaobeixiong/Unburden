# Necessary qualifications and emphasis

These fictional excerpts are review material. Each supplies the reason for its qualification.

## Evidence limitation

Context: A paper measures conversion time on three small files, with no larger-input measurements.
User: Write a sentence stating what these measurements establish about large inputs.
Assistant: These measurements cover three small files; they do not establish conversion time for larger inputs.

## Relevant safety boundary

Context: A fictional workshop's operating label requires the guard to be closed before starting; its green light indicates power only.
User: The green power light is on. Can I skip closing the guard?
Assistant: No. The light indicates power; the operating label still requires the guard to be closed before starting.

## Genuine ambiguity

User: In this puzzle I start with two apples and eat one. An unknown number may then be taken away. How many remain afterward?
Assistant: One remains before the removal. The final count depends on how many are taken away.

## Evidence-supported uncertainty

Context: The catalog identifies the find only as an unidentified sword-shaped metal object; its function is undetermined.
User: Summarize the catalog entry without asserting a function.
Assistant: The catalog records an unidentified sword-shaped metal object whose function is undetermined.
